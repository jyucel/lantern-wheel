How to play

  1. Goal: empty the wheel so that every dock holds lanterns of a single color.
  2. Spin: drag the wheel or use the spin buttons. Every gondola moves one place, all at once.
  3. Tap a dock: if the gondola facing it carries a lantern, the lantern is dropped onto the dock. If the gondola is empty, the dock’s next lantern is lifted onto it.
  4. Towers are stacks: only the lantern nearest the wheel can leave. Piers are queues: lanterns leave in the order they arrived, and new ones join at the far end.
  5. Gates (colored frame) accept only their color. Docks have limited room.
  6. Moves are limited. Every spin and every tap costs one. Match par (the proven minimum) for three stars. A new ★★★ or a daily puzzle earns a hint.

Keyboard: ← → spin · 1–5 docks · Z undo · R restart · H hint
